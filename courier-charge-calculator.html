How are courier charges calculated?

Courier companies generally consider the parcel's chargeable weight, origin and destination, service level and additional services when calculating shipping charges.

Chargeable weight may be the actual weight or volumetric weight, depending on which measurement is applicable under the courier's pricing rules.

Actual weight vs volumetric weight

A large but lightweight parcel can occupy significant space in a delivery vehicle. Because of this, courier companies may use volumetric or dimensional weight when calculating charges.

You can use the ParcelBench Volumetric Weight Calculator before estimating your courier charge.

Why can the final courier price be different?

Actual prices can vary depending on the courier company, PIN-code serviceability, delivery zone, package size, fuel surcharge, GST, remote-area charges, COD and other optional services.
