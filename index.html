What is volumetric weight?

Volumetric weight, also called dimensional weight, represents the amount of space a parcel occupies compared with its physical weight.

A large lightweight box can therefore have a higher volumetric weight than its actual weight.

Volumetric weight formula

When dimensions are measured in centimetres:

Length × Width × Height ÷ Volumetric Divisor

Example: a parcel measuring 40 × 30 × 20 cm using a divisor of 5000 has a volumetric weight of:

40 × 30 × 20 ÷ 5000 = 4.8 kg

Actual weight vs volumetric weight

Actual weight is the weight measured on a weighing scale. Volumetric weight is calculated from the parcel's dimensions.

Shipping providers may use the higher of actual weight and volumetric weight when determining chargeable weight, depending on their service and rate rules.

Using inches

ParcelBench can also accept dimensions in inches. The calculator converts the dimensions before applying the selected centimetre-based volumetric divisor.

Important

Volumetric-weight rules and divisors can differ between courier companies, transport modes and services. Check the applicable courier's current rules before relying on the result for billing.
