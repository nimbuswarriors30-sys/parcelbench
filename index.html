What is volumetric weight?

A large but lightweight parcel can occupy more vehicle or aircraft space than its scale weight suggests. Couriers therefore often compare its actual weight with its volumetric (dimensional) weight and may charge using the higher value.

Formula: Length × Width × Height ÷ Divisor

How to use ParcelBench

  1. Measure the packed parcel's length, width and height in centimetres.
  2. Enter its actual weight in kilograms.
  3. Select the divisor specified by your courier.
  4. Calculate and compare actual versus volumetric weight.

Example

A 40 × 30 × 20 cm parcel using a divisor of 5000 has a volumetric weight of 4.8 kg. If its actual weight is 3.2 kg, the calculator shows 4.8 kg as the chargeable-weight reference.
