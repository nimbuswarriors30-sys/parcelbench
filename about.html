About ParcelBench

ParcelBench provides straightforward parcel, courier and shipping calculators designed to help users understand common shipping measurements before booking a shipment.

Our goal is to make routine parcel calculations fast, clear and accessible on both phones and computers.

Calculator results are informational estimates. Courier companies may use different divisors, rounding rules, minimum weights and pricing policies.
